About

Djaunt Audio is the work of one person: writing, performing, tracking, mixing, and mastering, on my own material and across the other acts I've been part of. How much of any given record is me shifts by act and by era — sometimes most of the parts, sometimes just the engineering chair — but the same ear runs through all of it, which is why these records sound like they belong together even when the music doesn't.

What's here now is the finished output: the acts, the records, the streaming links. What's coming next is the process behind it — the DAW sessions, plugin chains, and mix templates that made these records, so this repo grows from a showcase into something other engineers can actually use.

What I do

  • Songwriting & arrangement
  • Performance, multi-instrument
  • Recording & tracking
  • Mixing & mastering
  • Sound design
  • Production, across acts
