About

Djaunt Audio is the work of one person: tracking, editing, mixing, and mastering, start to finish, on my own material and on records for the other acts I work with. No band of engineers, no outside mix house — one set of ears on everything, which is why the records under this roof sound like they belong together even when the music doesn't.

What's here now is the finished output: the acts, the records, the streaming links. What's coming next is the process behind it — the DAW sessions, plugin chains, and mix templates that made these records, so this repo grows from a showcase into something other engineers can actually use.
